# Constitution prompt

Read `docs/decisions.md`, `docs/requirements.md`, `docs/architecture.md`, `docs/roadmap.md`, `docs/open-questions.md`, `hardware/README.md` and `PROJECT_FOUNDATION.md`. The foundation is historical: current user decisions select a single pump RC input on ESP32-C3 with Cytron MD20A and native ESP-IDF for v1. The WROOM preference and illustrative pins in foundation are superseded. The existing `.specify/memory/constitution.md` is an unratified upstream template, not an existing approved constitution.

Use the installed Spec Kit constitution workflow to author an English constitution grounded in these agreed principles:

1. Keep v1 bounded to one local pump channel. Preserve fixed 1000–2000 us to 0–100 percent mapping and 1000 Hz output, without automatic endpoint calibration. Keep Wi-Fi/Bluetooth disabled and avoid network services. Future ignition ON/OFF, other ESP32 boards and Nano are separate deferred iterations.
2. Make safe startup, signal validity/freshness, explicit arming/recovery, fail-off and watchdog responsibilities visible. Invalid input must not become maximum output through blind clamping. Diagnostics must not delay control or cutoff. Hardware bias and independent emergency power cutoff are separate from software shutdown; MD20A PWM LOW means brake, not electrical isolation.
3. Keep a portable standard-C++ core independent of ESP-IDF and GPIO. Use explicit board profiles and platform adapters; native ESP-IDF for ESP32 and a separately selected build for a future concrete Nano model. Do not claim unsupported board/peripheral compatibility.
4. Make builds reproducible with selected and pinned SDK/toolchain dependencies. Require meaningful behavior tests and traceability from requirements to acceptance. Separate host tests, target build evidence and physical acceptance; never claim an unperformed measurement or completed milestone.
5. Follow the roadmap with bounded iterations. Prompts in `speckit-iteration/` are inputs; generated artifacts belong in `specs/<iteration>/` with explicit `SPECIFY_FEATURE_DIRECTORY`. Invoke only the phase requested. Do not create/switch branches, commit, push, flash or operate loads automatically. Preserve user changes.
6. Record decisions, uncertainty and changes honestly. Q01–Q09 belong to their owning iterations; resolve blocking choices before implementing the affected behavior. Do not silently adopt 100 ms timeout, 1400/1600 us ignition thresholds, shared two-channel cutoff, a board pinout or electrical suitability. Deferred questions must not block unrelated v1 work.

Document governance, amendment/version rules, adoption date and requirement/plan compliance checks. Synchronize affected upstream templates only as required by the constitution workflow, keeping a sync-impact report; do not add generic web/API rules, mandatory test-first approval cycles or unrequested feature work. Documentation and operator instructions are Ukrainian; prompts and constitution are English. Do not rewrite PROJECT_FOUNDATION.md. Stop after constitution adoption; do not invoke Specify, Plan, Tasks or Implement or mark firmware as implemented.
